Campaign guardrails

The system is built to protect sender reputation and prevent embarrassing double-taps before volume goes up.

Qualified only Every row needs a source URL and a short reason-fit note before a first-touch email is drafted.
Duplicate blocked The contact ledger suppresses prior sent, bounced, form-submitted, and source-URL duplicates across channels.
Reply triaged Interested, later, wrong-person, bounced, and no-fit replies get a clear owner and next action.
Scale after signal Run a small proof batch first, then only increase volume after reply quality and bounce rate are known.

GHL handoff map

The goal is not a fancy spreadsheet. It is a small system where qualified replies become opportunities that Arham or an operator can work immediately.

Lead Queue

Agency, owner, source, offer angle, reason fit, and exclusion checks.

Send Guard

Check email and source URL against the ledger before every send or form action.

Reply Label

Interested, later, wrong-person, no-fit, and bounced labels keep the inbox usable.

GHL Opportunity

Interested replies get offer, source, need, and next-step notes in the right pipeline stage.
