Inputs reviewed

Example funnel: a roofing contractor lead-generation campaign using fast AI follow-up as the main promise.

Ad promise

Book qualified roofing leads this week with an AI follow-up system that calls, texts, and routes every form fill into your CRM.

Landing page

Grow your business with modern marketing. We help contractors get more calls with proven strategies and automation. Schedule a consultation to learn more.

Target action

Book a demo call with enough confidence that the visitor understands what happens after submitting the form.

Findings

The page has a good service category, but it loses the specific buying intent created by the ad.

Message mismatch

The ad sells AI speed-to-lead, calling, texting, CRM routing, and urgency this week. The page retreats to broad marketing language.

Missing proof near the CTA

No workflow screenshot, response-time claim, mini case study, call log, or before-and-after result appears before booking.

Consultation expectation risk

The CTA does not explain who calls, how fast, what gets reviewed, or what the buyer receives from the call.

Lead routing risk

The ad says every form fill is routed into CRM, but the page has no visible trust signal around tracking, tagging, duplicate prevention, or response logging.

No urgency transfer

The ad says this week. The page does not preserve that time box or offer a fast-start path.

Category fit is salvageable

The page already says contractors, calls, and automation. The fix is sharper above-the-fold copy, proof, and routing clarity.

48-hour fix queue

The sprint output is designed to move directly into a landing-page editor, task board, or client report.

1

Rewrite the first screen around the exact ad promise

Use the words roofing leads, AI follow-up, calls, texts, CRM routing, and this week before the first CTA.
2

Add one proof block before the CTA

Use a workflow screenshot, call-response timeline, sample CRM record, or short result statement.
3

Replace vague CTA language

Change learn more and consultation language to Book a 15-minute demo of the roofing follow-up flow.
4

Clarify the post-submit path

State when the buyer hears back, what gets reviewed, and how leads are routed or tagged.
5

Add tracking and routing QA

Confirm form submission, CRM update, source attribution, duplicate handling, and failure notification.
